Kova {{ .Chart.AppVersion }} is installed in namespace {{ include "kova.namespace" . }}.

Check the rootless BuildKit workers:

  kubectl -n {{ include "kova.namespace" . }} rollout status deployment/{{ include "kova.fullname" . }}
  kubectl -n {{ include "kova.namespace" . }} get pods,service

  tcp://{{ include "kova.fullname" . }}.{{ include "kova.namespace" . }}.svc:{{ .Values.service.port }}

  http://{{ include "kova.fullname" . }}-service.{{ include "kova.namespace" . }}.svc:{{ .Values.serviceDaemon.service.port }}
